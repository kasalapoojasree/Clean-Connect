About CleanConnect

CleanConnect is a community platform where citizens report cleanliness problems with a photo and location, volunteers sign up for local activities, and administrators manage complaints from a single dashboard with a live map and analytics.

An image classifier suggests the waste type for each photo. It is an assistive prediction only, and each result states whether it came from a trained model or from the built-in demo fallback.

  1. A citizen reports an issue with a photo and location.
  2. The AI module suggests the waste category.
  3. An administrator reviews, assigns and resolves it.
  4. The citizen follows progress; the map and charts update.
