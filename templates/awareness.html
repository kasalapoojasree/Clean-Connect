Plastic pollution

Plastic breaks into tiny pieces but never fully disappears. It blocks drains, harms animals that swallow it, and ends up in rivers and lakes.

What you can do
  • Carry a cloth bag and a reusable bottle.
  • Refuse single-use cutlery, straws and thin carry bags.
  • Keep plastic dry and clean so it can be recycled.
  • Never burn plastic; it releases toxic fumes.

Clean water bodies

Lakes and ponds recharge groundwater, cool the surrounding area and support fish and birds. Dumped waste and sewage make the water unsafe and cause bad smells and mosquitoes.

What you can do
  • Do not throw puja waste, plastic or debris into water.
  • Report dumping near lakes using CleanConnect.
  • Join a water-body cleaning drive as a volunteer.
  • Keep drains free of waste so it does not wash into lakes.

Community responsibility

Clean streets are a shared effort. Authorities collect and process waste, but citizens decide how much waste is created and whether it is separated.
